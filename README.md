TraceRisk

TraceRisk is a privacy and security risk management platform designed to give users a clear view of the risks associated with their connected online accounts. The platform brings account security, privacy exposure, and potential breach risks into one centralized dashboard, allowing users to understand their current risk and take steps to reduce it.

The project includes:

A privacy dashboard showing account exposure and overall risk.
Account linking and management with associated permissions.
A breach simulator that visualizes how a compromise can propagate across connected accounts.
A risk network showing relationships between accounts.
A personalized Reduce Risk section with actionable security checklists.
Two-factor authentication and password-reuse risk identification.
Password security guidance and requirements.
High-risk account identification and recommended actions.
Account settings, notifications, and profile management.
A contact section for reporting issues and difficulties.

The overall aim of TraceRisk is to make digital privacy and security risks easier to understand by turning complex account relationships and vulnerabilities into clear, visual, and actionable information.
